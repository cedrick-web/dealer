# Product and security blueprint

## Differentiation
- Evidence-backed statuses state exactly what was checked, reviewer, date, source, limits and expiry.
- Transaction case links owner, buyer/tenant, agent, viewings, offers, signed fee terms, milestones, invoices and disputes.
- Commission provenance tracks lead attribution, payer, rate, due event, invoice and dispute state.
- Fraud triage flags duplicate images/contacts, suspicious prices, repeated parcel identifiers and stale listings for human review, never automatic accusations.
- Private participant-only chat, controlled evidence access, read receipts, reports and exportable case history.
- Kinyarwanda-first low-bandwidth UI, compressed media and opt-in notifications.
- Rental lifecycle, agent performance/appeals, nationwide district and sector taxonomy, AI-assisted duplicate detection.

## Verification
Identity check is not authority to sell. Document review is not title verification. A site visit is not ownership proof. Official Rwanda voluntary-sale guidance requires the parcel UPI to correspond to the seller and recommends checking with the Sector Land Manager when uncertain. Do not claim official database integration without authorization. Escalate inheritance, co-ownership, caveats and disputes to qualified professionals.

## Blockchain
Anchor only hashes of canonical evidence records, pseudonymous case IDs and timestamps. Keep identity documents, title PDFs, chats and private location off-chain. Hashes detect altered bytes; they do not prove truth. Solidity source is not deployed until audited and deployed. Consider a permissioned ledger before public chain anchoring.

## Revenue
Agent SaaS; clearly labelled sponsored placement; disclosed fixed listing/media/verification coordination fees; success commission under signed terms; professional referrals. Do not hold property funds without legal review and a licensed payment partner. Review BNR payment-services licensing and escrow requirements first.

## Security
Data minimization, consent, retention/deletion, encryption, private object storage, short-lived upload URLs, malware scanning, role and case-level authorization, admin MFA, rate limits, sensitive-access audit logs, backups and incident response. Review Rwanda data protection and cross-border hosting obligations.

## Release gates
Authentication/authorization tests, private case access tests, listing lifecycle and fee tests, audit integrity, dependency scans, backup restore drills, monitoring, legal review and authorized land/payment integrations.
